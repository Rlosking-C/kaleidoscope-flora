## Kaleidoscope World Liquor - the creative tab's section column

The section column in this mod's creative tab (the buttons down the left that
switch between Flower Drinks and Other) follows the design of **Kaleidoscope
World Liquor** by **"111"**. Its code is MIT and its assets are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
Only that MIT code-level design is reused: the two-section model, the placement
and the select-then-refresh flow. None of its assets are shipped here or in any
built jar.
