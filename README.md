# Brew drinks from flowers, and let every flower have a flavor of its own.

![ICON](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/02b8a0427f43e4231dcc0a6262afe492ae2ca20c.png)

Kaleidoscope Flora is an addon for **Kaleidoscope Cookery** that turns vanilla flowers into a full brewing experience. Pick a flower, work it into a tea bag, brew it — and drink the flower's blessing.

## Signature drinks

- **When the Wind Rises** — finish the cup mid-fall. You will not land the way you expected.

- **Rosy Stride** — cross open water on a film of petals. Fifty blocks in one unbroken stride is the test.

- **Hanami Tale** — a veil of petals that stops arrows. Five projectiles in a single cup.

- **Breath of the Ancients** — see the world the way a sniffer smells it, and dig pottery sherds out of the ground.

- **Echo of the End** — once per cup, a killing blow is answered with a blink instead of a death screen.

- **The Sunward** — one form by day, a light source by night.

- **Vernal Awakening** — ripen fifty crops in one go.

- **Voracious Urn** — everything drops twice, if you are willing to gamble on it.

- **Lullaby** — fall asleep drowsy and wake at dawn with the dream still on you.

- **The Gaze** — pin an enderman where it stands. The abyss does not stare back.

…and many more, each bound to the flower it is brewed from.

## What's inside

- **26 flower drinks** — each with its own recipe, its own effect, and a flower-language quote to read while you drink

- **27 status effects** — nothing here is a recolored vanilla buff

- **20 advancements** — from your first sip to drinking every drink there is

- **A brewing chain** — flowers become tea bags, tea bags go into the teapot

- **Two foods with tricks** — flower pies (one is throwable and pins what it hits, one lands with a shockwave) and blossom mooncakes (eat one and swim through the air for a hundred blocks)

- **The Infusion Ledger** — an in-game guidebook covering every drink, its effect, and where its flower grows (requires Patchouli)

- **One teapot yields 12 cups** — pour them into Cookery's empty teacups

## How it plays

Flowers are not decoration here — they are ingredients with consequences. Every drink is a small trade: a movement tool, a combat trick, a farming aid, or a way out of a bad situation. Finding the flower that solves your problem is the whole loop.

## Dependencies

**Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge** and **Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge** are both supported.

- **Required:** [Kaleidoscope Cookery](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/kaleidoscope-cookery) 1.6.0

- **Optional:**
  - [Patchouli](https://modrinth.com/mod/patchouli) — enables The Infusion Ledger
  - [Sodium Dynamic Lights](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sodium-dynamic-lights) — full glow for The Sunward
  - [Vanilla Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vanilla-backport) — unlocks 4 crossover drinks brewed from newer flowers
  - [Immortalers Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immortalers-delight) — extra finds while Breath of the Ancients is active
